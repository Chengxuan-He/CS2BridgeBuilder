## Scenarios

Each of 17 x 11, 7 x 9 and 32 x 24 output dimensions used nonconstant synthetic
13 x 7 source textures with generated mips, repeat wrapping and trilinear
sampling. All four authored control channels varied. The probe independently
requires at least four distinct control-output pixels. The first two output
sizes exercise partial 8 x 8 thread groups.

The probe used valid `SurfaceAtlas` bindings, `OriginalRoad = 0`, a nontrivial
patch rectangle, and linear ARGB32 random-write render targets. Readback used
RGBA32 `ReadPixels`, without material baking or texture encoding.

For every size it checked:

- A fresh shader instance with `SkipAtlasControl` never assigned: all four
  channels were byte-identical to the old shader.
- `SkipAtlasControl = 1`: three fresh primary output targets matched the
  baseline; the separately initialized 1 x 1 control UAV retained its exact
  sentinel bytes.
- The same shader instance switched from 1 to 0, with four fresh full-size
  targets: every channel matched the old baseline, including authored control.

Fresh targets prevent stale preceding output from masking a skipped dispatch.
The scope is exact output-byte equivalence in the production ARGB32 target
format on this D3D11 device. This does not claim a GPU timing improvement,
cross-driver bit equivalence, or exhaustive coverage of every native-road
branch. The unchanged sampling expressions plus the isolated guard are the
source-level basis for other branches. Existing signedness/loop-shadow shader
warnings occurred in both comparison variants; no shader compilation failure
or probe exception occurred.
