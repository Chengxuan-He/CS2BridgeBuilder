Existing immutable products are retained in the private Lift01 environment under
`structure-semantic/unit/`. Four source-CID variants resolve to three distinct
native descriptors sharing three channel payloads and transparent control. They
were not rebaked. The complete game descriptor reader passed for all three
descriptors. Durable evidence is copied to the public artifact directory as
`steel-bake-report.json`, `steel-native-check.json`, and `steel-descriptors.log`.

The measured candidate spans 103 by 369 original source pixels and approximately
2.2479248046875 by 7.98876953125 metres. All 27 channel/mip readback cases passed
the original extraction tolerance; maximum linear reconstruction error is
0.0021972954273223877. No source-density reduction is reported.

These encoding checks **do not establish a genuine source repeat period**. Zero
output native Repeat error is a property of the resulting wrapping texture, not
proof that the original authored steel region is periodic. Original-atlas filtered
boundary differences reach 0.01708984375 for BaseColor, 0.007843166589736938 for
Normal, and 0.2649881839752197 for Mask. The last difference is material and remains
unresolved. The original RGB channel diagnostic suggests smooth coating, but visual
absence of bolts or grating is insufficient all-channel/mip periodicity evidence.
The proposed broader coverage rectangle was never registered.

Do not integrate this convenient source crop, infer periodicity from output
wrapping, or defer known unsupported sampling to human acceptance. Proceed only
with independently evidenced genuine source units and accepted native filtered
boundary behavior, or another contract-compliant representation. Do not restore
the superseded per-sampler structural candidates.
