# Draw01 fixed central front cap fragments

## Measured scope

Draw01FixedFrontFragmentData replaces far main-mesh faces 116/117/276/277 and
442/443/600/601. The corresponding high/middle-detail center support meets the carriageway
underside at Y=9.5. The far mesh aggregates the old road into its pier caps up to
Y=9.800048828125. Retain the native cap below the measured underside boundary and replace
the upper road portion with the selected-road construction.

Sixteen edge vertices create twelve retained triangles. The far native Z planes remain
+/-83.708984375; this does not move the coarse pier to the high-detail Z=85.892578125 plane.
Original vertices are unchanged. Near and middle LODs require no fragments from this table,
as their road slab and underside are already covered by whole-face removal data.

Native edge normal/tangent/bone bytes are identical, and the retained triangles preserve
native winding. Bone remains 0, material slot 0, with new UVs interpolated in Float16.
No material or texture is generated by the cap replacement. Source Geometry identity is
embedded in the generated class.

## Integration

Append Vertices to the shared plan's edge vertices and pass the absolute first index to
Faces. Combine the table with rear caps, moving caps, whole-road masks and remaining fixed
structure plans; do not construct a separate overlapping mesh for each fragment table.

Higher sidewalk inner walls and front sidewalk caps are separate authored surfaces and must
not be lowered by this center-cap table. Their full correspondence, widening, selected-road
partition and final generator assembly remain unfinished. Native near/far and movement
acceptance requires human-created samples; offline cap inspection is not that acceptance.
