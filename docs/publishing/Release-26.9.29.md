# Bridge Builder 26.9.29

## English
- Fixed copied prefab identities using bridge UUIDs and copy names.
- Added safe cleanup of failed bridge prefabs and their placed networks after loading.
- Fixed tower/mesh asset-name collisions affecting double-deck Golden Gate towers.
- Lower roads on double-deck Golden Gate bridges now use the localized bridge name.

Known issue: double-deck railway switches remain unresolved and are deferred to the next release.
