# Arc pedestrian bridge implementation

## Source and construction

The base-game `Arc Pedestrian Bridge` and the Bridge Expansion Pack revised variant use the same `Pedestrian Arc Bridge Piece`. This implementation records the base-game arrangement, without requiring the original authoring network Prefab. The native piece is the catalogue resource/provenance anchor.

The geometry resource is `868884d0d55c3ec427cf44fdc8d4a322`, entry `PedestrianBridgeArc_94f3c5fdebfe4964b9c2d33e236ff9a5.Geometry` in `Content/Game/Blob.cok`. Its original Surface is `5d2454994f1ec785bae61f9314cd0975`. Native geometry has 4,818 vertices and 4,960 triangles in one mesh. The original piece has no external LOD references; the same native representation remains the reference at all viewing distances. No high-detail geometry is substituted for an existing reduced-detail representation.

The measured native road width is 3 m and the nominal overhead piece width is 6 m. Actual decoded geometry extends from -2.4000244140625 to +2.4000244140625 m. The nominal catalogue width and actual geometry envelope are deliberately distinguished. Native bridge segment length is 32 m, water elevation 5 m, no hanging or curvature, and no fixed segments. The original centered pedestrian pillar remains at (0, -3, 0), anchored at nodes and requiring elevation. The selected road retains its own pavement, lanes, markings and sidewalk layout.

## Width mapping

`tools/GeometryMetaprogram/ArcPedestrian.py` decodes the original native resource and performs identification offline. Its 71 welded islands separate into 64 rigid side members and seven transverse crossbeams. The side members include complete arches, longitudinal deck-adjacent beams, rods and connection fittings. None crosses the centre; each moves rigidly by signed half-width delta. Each crossbeam uses its own measured reach. The runtime receives only the vertex-ordered displacement coefficients and derivative coefficients in two BBN1 resources. It performs no spatial classification.

The shared geometry factory evaluates the packed affine program, retains original topology and raw attributes, and transforms packed normal/tangent frames for the seven scaled crossbeams. Rigid frames remain unchanged. Original structural Surface and atlas UV coordinates remain native; transverse finite artwork stretches with its corresponding beam, so this is not a claim of constant transverse texel density. No structural textures or replacement road materials are packaged. The standalone centered support is not widened.

## Verification and limitations

Offline checks passed at road widths 3, 8, 16 and 32 m: original-width positional identity, unchanged Y/Z, rigid side displacement, and zero inconsistent displacement assignments at coincident attribute seams. The source and widened wireframe were inspected; complete arches, rods and transverse beams remain present. Measurements and the comparison image are retained under `artifacts/four-new-styles/ArcPedestrian/`.

These checks do not establish native creation, preview, near/far appearance, loading or the measured generated-owner width invariant. A human-created bridge sample, in-game near/far inspection and cold load remain required. The native selected-road pipeline owns zero/one/asymmetric sidewalks; the recorded overhead mesh contains structural arches and side beams, not an added prototype pavement strip. Common localization, compilation, packaging and deployment checks are reported by the integrating agent.

## Reported preview structure investigation

The reported preview at road width 22.5 m shows a white rectangular extension behind one road end, thin arches and four tall round columns. The corresponding log requests are `tmp127a2324-31fb-49c9-93ff-0b23ea22dca0` and `tmpaf4fe805-7bbc-4b7f-a060-fa40c2a3db8a`. Both use selected road `r10f81fc0-7603-440d-ad80-7912c2c6e72a-76561199197854251`; the measured elevated branch has no sidewalks. Successful preview completion in the log does not certify its appearance.

Fresh decoding of the native geometry gives SHA-256 `d06b48acdddc2435e9c372ba1e31a91ab3d43a4a43e8fe400c041b885b43bf14`. Applying the checked-in displacement resource at this exact width produces X bounds [-12.1500244140625, 12.1500244140625] m, Y bounds [-0.10006523132324219, 11.69921875] m and Z bounds [-32, 32] m. All 4,818 source vertices and 4,960 triangles remain; Y/Z are bit-identical and every side member moves exactly 9.75 m. The solid offline comparison (`artifacts/four-new-styles/ArcPedestrian/arc-solid-22_5.png`) contains arches, rods and seven crossbeams, with no rectangular panel. Its machine-readable companion is `ArcPedestrian-preview-geometry-audit.json`. This excludes a panel in the recorded structural source/mapping; it does not identify which native preview draw produced the reported panel.

The anatomy dump records `Vertical Pillar Pedestrian` as a replacement for `Pillar Pedestrian Placeholder`, with `m_Circular = True`. Its main mesh has bounds X [-0.95, 0.95], Y [-1, 1.000001], Z [-0.7, 0.7] m and `StackProperties` set to Middle/Up. The preview presents the deck 30 m above its test ground. The round columns and thin arches are authentic source features; the catalogue illustration is not an exact support-selection oracle.

The common preview uses native `GenerateCompositionMesh`, which normalizes source X/Z against composition width and half the piece length. The recorded Arc length is 64 m and its source Z bounds are [-32, 32] m; no additional style-specific normalization is appropriate. Earlier common repairs added non-rendered continuation edges and unified native course/lane placement. Those changes did not resolve the reported white panel.

The subsequent actual Arc capture `e732c0035acc476fb42a1ac10e30b427`, request `tmp3354aedc-b9af-4b6d-b5a1-dc0581bc4d29`, identifies the panel conclusively. Draws 125–220 instantiate `WindowSecurityShuttersLarge01 Mesh` 96 times: two occurrences of the 48-mesh third-party horizontal-pillar candidate `楼梯玻璃棚 -高铁站`. Their combined world bounds are X [-12.0630491, 11.90595145], Y [-1.27548811, -1.27451119], Z [1.25597618, 52.36802617] m. These nearly horizontal panels extend over 20 m beyond the visible course, whose Z range is [0, 32]. This is an actual captured support-object draw, not a conclusion inferred from the absence of a panel in the Arc source. The earlier capture whose request was only `Arc` matched `GOArch` and is not evidence for this incident.

The same captured replacement explains the detached supports. Its native `PillarObject` offset range is [7.5, 17.5], so the common selector placed the four genuine pedestrian columns at X = ±12.5, Z = 0 and 32, with their tops at Y = -3. It selected the station canopy instead of the connecting pedestrian crossbeam. The original placeholder Y = -3 is retained: native `Pillar Pedestrian Top 21` and `Top 22` have upper Y = 2, completing the connection to the original elevated path underside around Y = -1. Moving the columns upward by 3 m would conceal the wrong selection rather than reproduce native assembly.

The selection error comes from two different native width definitions. `BridgePreviewScene` passed `course.EdgeData.m_Width`, including the Edge-only 25.5 m Arc overhead piece. Native `NetInitializeSystem` computes `NetGeometryData.m_ElevatedWidth` using only `Elevated`, without `Edge`; this excludes the Arc piece and gives the selected road's 22.5 m width. `SubObjectSystem` and `AlignSystem.SelectHorizontalPillar` score candidates against that elevated width minus 1 m. The erroneous target 24.5 gives the 24.1260978 m station canopy score 0.728394, beating `Pillar Pedestrian Top 25` at 0.668485. At the native target 21.5, `Top 21` and `Top 22` both score 0.668485, while the canopy scores only 0.276319. All these candidates have probability 100. No name-based exclusion of third-party assets is required.

Retained evidence is under `artifacts/four-new-styles/ArcPedestrian/`: `ArcPedestrian-capture-diagnosis.json`, `ArcPedestrian-capture-shutter-draws.json` and `ArcPedestrian-pillar-candidates.json`. The candidate table records every horizontal replacement in the inspected anatomy dump, with authored dimensions, offset ranges, probabilities and both scores. The independently saved owner `b3c0a06b5-dc94-4a33-b486-dc1552020c07` also decodes to the expected 4,818 vertices and 4,960 triangles with the measured widened Arc bounds; its recorded placeholder remains at Y = -3.

The shared correction passes `course.Network.m_ElevatedWidth` to support selection. It changes preview assembly, not the Arc recipe or saved assets. This diagnosis establishes the actual faulty draw and selection cause; deployment and a new in-game preview are still required to establish that the panel disappears and native crossbeams connect both pairs of columns. Near/far appearance and cold-load acceptance remain separate checks.

This investigation changes documentation and retained diagnostic artifacts only. No user-visible strings or localization routes change; no Arc geometry recipe or native asset is modified.
